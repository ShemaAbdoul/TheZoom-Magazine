About The Zoom

We get close to the stories that matter, then pull back far enough to see why.

The Zoom Magazine is an independent publication covering technology, business, culture and sport. Every story starts with a simple question: what does this look like up close?

  • TechBuilders, tools and the ideas behind them.
  • BusinessSmall shops, big plans and honest numbers.
  • CultureMusic, art and the places people gather.
  • SportLocal pitches, rising players and the coaches behind them.
